For this week's homework I wanted to experiment with simulating space and a world (similar to Epitaph and Emily Short's The Annals of the Parrigues). Unlike both of those works I wanted to try providing more wayfinding too. I'm a nerd for maps, so I thought it could be interesting to build a map as someone explores the simulated space.

A Tracery grammar generates a new location whenever traveling to a new location, and in this version, re-visiting existing locations does not effect the previously generated place.
