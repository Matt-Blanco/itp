## Apart and together again

For this week I learned how hard it is to find a simple mechanical toy. It took visiting a couple of stores before I found one that I foudn interesting (and it still had batteries included). I found a mechanical toy car shaped as a dinosaur that when pressed down spins the back wheels on their own. There's also a small switch to turn on four LEDs in the wheels.
